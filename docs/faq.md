# AL-Naqawa FAQ

Checked 2026-10-30. For service changes, consult the [live capability page](https://alnaqawa.com/en/mcp) and discover the current tool schemas.

## What is AL-Naqawa?

AL-Naqawa is an English-first website offering calculated prayer times and related tools. Its public beta MCP service gives compatible AI clients access to calculations and timetable generation. This repository documents that service and its interpretation.

## Is it a mobile app or a clock I can buy?

No released mobile app or physical clock is represented by this repository. The website's [About page](https://alnaqawa.com/en/about) describes those as development plans. Do not treat concept images or future features as released products.

## Does MCP require an AL-Naqawa API key?

The public beta currently does not. Client compatibility, request limits and service availability still apply. Access to an AI client may have its own requirements or charges.

## Does the service support every city?

The catalog is being published in stages. Search for an available record, verify the country and time zone, and preserve an explicit not-found error. Do not silently use a neighbouring or same-name city.

## Are the results mosque Iqamah times?

They are calculated starts. A mosque sets its own congregation and Friday schedules. Contact the mosque or consult its own timetable for those details.

## Which calculation method should I follow?

The method tool lists available presets, and the comparison tool helps explain their output differences. Neither establishes a universally preferable religious practice. Consult the guidance relevant to your community and retain the settings in any shared result.

## Can the tools set an alarm or email a timetable?

They return data and files. They do not send messages, configure phone alarms or create events inside an account. Calendar import is separate, and its behaviour should be checked in the receiving application.

## Does an ICS file contain prayer durations?

The published workflow describes calculated start markers, not a claimed duration for performing a prayer. It does not include a built-in alarm. Review calendar defaults and any imported events.

## Is Qibla output a live compass?

It is an initial bearing measured clockwise from true north. It does not read a device's magnetic sensor. Keep that distinction when using a separate physical or phone compass.

## Is a calculated Hijri date an official announcement?

No. A calendar method supplies calculated date labels for planning. Local moon-sighting and observance announcements can differ. Retain the method and any day adjustment.

## Does this repository guarantee AI citations?

No. Clear documentation, evidence and accessible pages help readers inspect a claim. They do not guarantee indexing, rankings, model training or citations. Google's [AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) does not require a special AI file or special schema for inclusion.
